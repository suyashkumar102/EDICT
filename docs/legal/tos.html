EDICT — Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-05-25 · Applies to all installations of the EDICT Devvit app.

1. What EDICT is

EDICT is a moderation tool installed by subreddit moderators on Reddit via Devvit (Reddit’s developer platform). It compiles English-language moderation rules into a deterministic decision tree, evaluates them against posts and comments submitted to the subreddit where it is installed, and either records the result (shadow mode) or carries it out via Reddit’s moderator APIs (live mode).

2. Who can use it

Only subreddit moderators (verified by Reddit’s permission model) may install EDICT, compose or activate rules, or invoke its menu actions. EDICT performs an additional moderator re-check on every request before executing any state-changing action.

3. Data EDICT handles

  • Rule text and metadata. The plain-English sentences a moderator types, the resulting compiled JSON, and the audit log of rule lifecycle events. Stored in Devvit’s installation-scoped Redis. EDICT does not transmit rule text off the platform except to OpenAI (see §5).
  • Per-decision audit entries. When a rule fires, EDICT records the matched clause, captured fact-bag values, and the action taken. Audit entries are kept for the configured rollback window (default 30 days) and an additional 90-day analytics tail.
  • Reversal patterns. When a moderator reverses an EDICT action, the matching fact-pattern is hashed (DJB2) and the hash is stored to deprioritize similar future matches. The original post and comment IDs are not stored alongside the hash.

4. Actions EDICT can take

EDICT can report, flair, lock, sendToModQueue, approve, sticky, distinguish, commentReply, and modmailNotify by default. Higher-impact actions (remove, mute, ban, contributor add/remove) are gated behind an explicit per-rule opt-in at compile time and may additionally require a second moderator’s consensus.

5. AI provider (OpenAI)

EDICT calls OpenAI’s API only at rule-edit time — never on user-submitted post or comment content. The compiler receives the English sentence the moderator wrote plus EDICT’s schema, and returns a structured JSON rule. No post or comment body is ever sent to OpenAI. The API key is stored as a Devvit secret and is visible only to subreddit moderators with secret-set permission.

6. Liability and warranty

EDICT is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind. Moderation decisions remain the responsibility of the moderators who installed and configured the app. The author(s) of EDICT accept no liability for actions taken, missed, or reversed by the app.

7. Removing the app

A moderator may uninstall EDICT at any time from the subreddit’s app management page. Uninstallation halts all rule evaluation immediately. Past audit entries persist in the installation’s Redis until the next rollback-window sweep.
