# EDICT brand identity

## Logomark

A stylised "E" composed of three horizontal strokes that lengthen
top-to-bottom — visually echoing how a multi-clause rule fans out
from a single English sentence. The bottom stroke extends past the
others to form an underline, suggesting _signature_ and _finality_
(an edict, after all, is signed and binding).

Colour: `#0E7C66` (emerald slate). Renders cleanly on Reddit's light
and dark themes without contrast adjustments.

Sizing:

- `Logomark.png` — 256×256 for the App Directory listing
- `Logomark-16.png` — 16×16 favicon
- `Logomark-512.png` — 512×512 for marketing

## Wordmark

The wordmark is the full word `EDICT` in a geometric sans, kerned
slightly wider than default for legibility at small sizes. The `I`
is mildly elongated to balance the rectangular block of `E-D-C-T`.

## Voice

Direct, technical, mildly playful. Sentences are short. We never use
words like "powerful" or "intelligent" to describe the product —
those are claims the product itself should prove.

We refer to moderators as moderators, not "users" or "you." A mod
opening the app is the most important reader of every line of UI
copy.

## Naming taglines

Primary: **"Speak it. Sandbox it. Send it. Undo it."**

The four-verb structure mirrors the four-stage acronym (English-
Declared, Inspected, Compiled, Traced) without being explicit. Each
verb maps to a layer:

- _Speak_ — the English input
- _Sandbox_ — the shadow phase
- _Send_ — the live phase
- _Undo_ — the reversal path

Secondary: _"Reddit moderation, fluent."_

Used in the Devpost submission and the README hero. Three words —
deliberate; we want to imply the language _is_ the interface, not
a trick the tool performs on top of it.

## Logo placement

- App Directory: `assets/Logomark.png` (256×256 PNG, transparent)
- Custom-post header: top-left, before the wordmark
- Command Center ribbon: large emerald text, no logomark needed
- Modmail signature: small wordmark, no logomark

Never use the logomark as an avatar inside the custom-post body;
it competes with the actual content. Always reserve it for the
ribbon header.
